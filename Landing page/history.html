History of Tukuran

The rich cultural heritage and natural beauty of this peaceful municipality.

Located in the province of Zamboanga del Sur, Philippines, Tukuran is a hidden gem for travelers seeking a peaceful and authentic Filipino experience. This charming municipality is surrounded by lush greenery, rolling hills, and pristine beaches, making it an ideal destination for nature lovers, families, and adventure seekers.

Tukuran is home to several remarkable natural attractions, including the picturesque Tukuran Waterfalls, where visitors can enjoy the refreshing crystal-clear waters while appreciating the breathtaking scenery. The municipality also features beautiful beaches with soft white sand, swaying palm trees, and clear turquoise waters, offering relaxing destinations for swimming, sightseeing, and family recreation.
